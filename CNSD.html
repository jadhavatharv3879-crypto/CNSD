$$P(Y_{ij} = 1) = \frac{1}{1 + \exp\big(-\alpha_j(\theta_i - \beta_j)\big)}$$

Pedagogical Breakthrough

The "Correct Answer Trap" Decoupling

In an empirical study of 20,964 authentic student answers from the Eedi mathematics platform, 1.6% of responses fall into the True Misconception regime: students select the correct numerical option via completely broken logic.

Example: Evaluating (-5) - (-8) Student writes: "I just did 8 - 5 = 3".
Numerical answer: +3 (Correct!)
Underlying logic: Completely flawed!

Conversely, standard binary evaluators penalize brief 3-word explanations, yielding a crushing 25.4% False Positive Rate. Our 4-tier rubric decouples the Answer Mark from the Method Mark, lowering FPR to 18.0% ($p < 0.001$).

1
Tier 1: Clear Deduction Answer: $y=y^*$ | Method: Valid

Advance learner mastery state $\mathbf{z}_t$, increment 2PL-IRT student proficiency parameter $\theta_i$.

2
Tier 2: Needs Clarification Answer: $y=y^*$ | Method: Brief (< 5 words)

Issue a non-punitive formative probe ("Show your intermediate step") without degrading mastery.

3
Tier 3: Hidden Misconception (Trap Catch!) Answer: $y=y^*$ | Method: Buggy Rule

Intercept unearned success! Triggers Module 2 to synthesize a targeted counter-example problem where this buggy operational rule fails.

4
Tier 4: Explicit Error Answer: $y \neq y^*$ | Method: Flawed

Executes full 3-stage Generate-Retrieve-Rerank diagnostic pipeline and invokes neuro-symbolic remediation loop.
